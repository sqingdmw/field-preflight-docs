# Field Preflight Support

Contact: sqingdmwc@gmail.com

Include the app name, browser, approximate time/time zone, operation attempted,
and sanitized error code or screenshot. State whether you are a Jira administrator.
Remove site URLs, account IDs, query literals and business data where unnecessary.
Never send passwords, API tokens, verification codes or an unredacted Automation
export/report. Ask before sending a diagnostic attachment containing sensitive data.

For a suspected vulnerability, use the same email with subject
"Field Preflight security report" and a minimal, sanitized reproduction. Do not
test another person's tenant or expose their data. We will review reports and
coordinate fixes/disclosure as appropriate. No response-time SLA or bounty is promised.

The initial release is free with best-effort support. Support does not include
destructive Jira changes, complete-discovery guarantees, recovery of unsaved
reports or a contractual uptime/response-time SLA.

Site administrators can uninstall through Atlassian Connected apps. Uninstalling
removes app site access; it does not delete files downloaded by users or support
correspondence sent separately. Delete local copies on your device and contact
support about support-data requests.

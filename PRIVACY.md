## App Data Processing

Field Preflight is a Jira Cloud app for signed-in Jira administrators. It reads
custom-field metadata, contexts, project/work-type associations, screens, exposed
workflow configuration, visible saved filters/JQL/owner metadata, associated
boards and visible work-item usage counts. Returned configuration may contain
personal data such as filter-owner account IDs/display names or names/query text.
End users do not supply API tokens or passwords to the app.

Requests use the Atlassian Forge bridge as the signed-in user on the current site.
The app does not send Jira report payloads to a developer-run API or external
analytics/AI service. Atlassian hosts and operates the platform; its processing,
logging and service policies also apply. This is not a claim that platform
services perform no logging or that no personal data is processed.

Optional Automation exports and baseline reports are selected by the user and
processed in the browser session. Automation content is not uploaded by this app
to a developer endpoint. Reports/comparisons may include extracted configuration
evidence, owner metadata and file hashes.

## Storage And Sharing

The app has no developer-operated report database or Forge report-storage
backend. Active report/import/comparison state is held in browser-session memory.
Closing or reloading discards unsaved state. Downloaded reports persist on users'
devices until users delete them. Lack of a database does not mean lack of
personal-data processing.

We do not sell app report data or add advertising trackers. If you send support
requests, we receive correspondence and material you choose to provide through
our email service. Send only necessary, sanitized information. Support information
is used to answer requests and address reliability/security issues, and retained
only as needed for these purposes or legal obligations.

Public documentation is hosted on GitHub. Visiting it is separate from running
the app. GitHub may process visitor information under its
[privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The app does not send report data to the documentation repository, which contains
approved public documentation, not private source code, tenant reports or credentials.

## Your Choices And Requests

Only install/use where authorized to access the Jira information. Review Atlassian
installation permissions. Site administrators can uninstall to remove future app
access. Delete downloaded files on your devices and ask your Jira administrator
about information retained by Atlassian. Contact the operator for access,
correction or deletion requests concerning support information held by the
publisher. Applicable rights depend on relevant law and the data role.

No legal certification, specific data-residency guarantee or blanket regulatory
compliance is claimed. Any required data-processing agreement and publisher legal
disclosures must be settled before the relevant customer use. Material policy
changes will be dated and published with the app documentation.
